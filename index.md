# Hello! 

Welcome to Prob Blog, a probability blog. Here, we present human-generated expositions on topics related to probability theory.

# Our Goal

The goal of Prob Blog is three-fold: to encourage human-generated mathematics content, to provide intuitive and introductory explanations for people seeking to learn more about probability, and to offer opportunities for young mathematicians to learn and explain new or familiar mathematical topics.     


# Why Human-generated?
Lately, mathematics has become a playing ground for large companies such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others, whose interest in mathematics is driven exclusively by their desire to showcase the ability of some new model is. Our position is quite simple: AI-generated mathematics content at its current growth rate has the potential to destroy the discipline. We will avoid writing an essay here, as "The crisis of AI-generated mathematics" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02859) makes many of our ideas explicit, but the general point is that if we follow the current trajectory, human understanding of mathematics and thus its usefulness will rapidly be destroyed. I, for one, don't want that, and thus this blog is a small effort towards making sure humans keep doing mathematics, and humans keep talking about it! For those in research, I strongly recommend reading about and joining The Association for Human Mathematics (AHM); see: https://www.ahmath.org/.
